**Status:** Draft  
**Layer:** L6 Creator  
**Depends on:** L2 Engine complete, L5 Community

- AI Builder (`/studio/build`)
- No-Code Builder (`/studio/create`)
- Remix, Template Marketplace, Logic Marketplace
- Creator Analytics, Revenue (L7 handoff)

- Per-game custom publish flows

Must pass 10-Gate in Master Task. Layer L6 — do not expand until L2 Multiplayer DoD checked.
